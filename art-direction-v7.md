# v7 Art Direction — Pastel Tsum Fantasy

- Rounded, plush, toy-like silhouettes; large expressive faces; tiny limbs.
- Pastel pink, lilac, mint, baby blue, peach and butter-yellow, with dark plum outlines for readability.
- AI-generated hero and creature source art is integrated through `assets/tsum-atlas.png`.
- Runtime motion uses squash-and-stretch, bobbing, facing flips, hit compression, elite glow, boss scale and attack telegraphs.
- Environment keeps 2.5D Y-depth, biome weather and lighting while moving toward softer candy-fantasy materials.
- Combat readability always wins over decorative density on mobile.
