# Pocket Starwing — Gameplay v2

## Core loop
Move, fire, dodge, graze enemy shots, break hazards, collect upgrades, thread terrain gaps, beat the sector guardian, then choose the next biome.

## Collision philosophy
Environmental collision is deliberate and readable. Terrain separates the ship out of solid geometry, applies knockback, damage, screen shake and a short invulnerability window so one mistake does not instantly drain the hull.

## Skill systems
- Directional dodge costs energy and grants a short invulnerability window.
- Grazing enemy projectiles restores energy and gives score.
- Cleanly passing through narrow terrain gaps awards a thread-the-needle bonus.
- Combo multiplier rises through kills and collapses when the player is hit.
- Destructible rocks and crystals can be shot to open safer lines and may drop power-ups.

## Biome hazards
- Cloudberry Run: wind lanes push the ship vertically.
- Ember Canyon: asteroid clusters create breakable moving cover.
- Moonlit Ruins: oscillating gates create timing challenges.
- Thunder Reach: lightning columns telegraph before becoming lethal.
- Candy Nebula: gravity wells pull the ship off-line.

## Combat variety
Enemy formations mix scouts, zig fighters, divers, turrets and tanks. Bosses use multiple projectile patterns and enter a faster rage phase below 45% health.

## Game-feel layer
Autofire, particles, collision knockback, hit-stop, screen shake, animated ship tilt, shield feedback, score popups and synthesized sound effects make hits and kills more legible and satisfying.
